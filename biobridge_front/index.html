How it works

Connected in an afternoon. Hands-off after that.

01

Connect Odoo

Server URL, database, login and an API key. On a multi-company Odoo, pick the company from the list.

02

Add your devices

A BioTime server, or a terminal by its IP. Name it "Front door", give it a location — the connection test registers it in Odoo.

03

Match badges

Badges match Odoo employees by barcode, PIN, registration number or work email. Anything unclear waits for you.

04

It syncs on its own

Every 5 minutes to every few hours, your choice. You only hear from BioBridge when something needs you.

Why BioBridge

Raw punches are messy. Your attendance shouldn't be.

People double-tap, forget to badge out, work past midnight — and half the terminals in the field have no IN/OUT keys. BioBridge handles all of it before Odoo sees a single record.

Three pairing modes

Alternating, trust-the-device, or first-in/last-out per day — with automatic fallback when a terminal stamps everything "Check In".

Guard rails, not surprises

Double-taps are dropped, night shifts stay one record, and a forgotten check-out is capped and flagged — never a 300-hour shift.

Nothing lost in an outage

Punches are captured first and pushed when Odoo is reachable. Odoo down for a day costs a delay, never data.

Knows which door

Each attendance carries the terminal it came from — on Odoo Online too, with no add-on to install.

Multi-company, multi-site

Several BioTime servers and standalone terminals side by side, each connection scoped to the right Odoo company.

New hires onto the device

Odoo employees with a badge ID or PIN are created on your ZKTeco terminal, ready to enrol their fingerprint.
